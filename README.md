## 🎯 Objective

Evaluate the relationship between **urban mobility and economic performance** across Latin American cities by analyzing:

- 🚗 Traffic congestion
- ⏱️ Travel times
- 💰 GDP per capita
- 📉 Unemployment
- 🌎 Differences between cities in the region

The goal was to explore whether higher congestion levels are associated with measurable economic patterns and identify cities that may require deeper infrastructure analysis.

---

## 👨‍💻 My Role

Developed the analytical workflow from **multi-source data to city-level insights**:

- Merged traffic and economic datasets from multiple sources
- Cleaned and standardized city-level data using **Python & Pandas**
- Performed **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** to identify patterns and anomalies
- Analyzed relationships between congestion indicators and economic variables
- Created visualizations to support the interpretation of mobility and economic trends
- Prepared and structured the analysis in **Jupyter Notebook**

---

## 🚀 Results & Impact

The analysis identified:

- A very weak overall relationship between traffic congestion and GDP per capita
- Significant differences in mobility and economic indicators across Latin American cities
- **Bogotá** as a city with relatively high congestion and lower economic productivity compared with some other cities in the analysis
- **Mexico City** as one of the cities with the highest average traffic times
- The need to evaluate congestion together with additional economic and infrastructure variables rather than treating traffic as an isolated indicator

The findings suggest that congestion alone does not explain economic performance, but city-level comparisons can help identify locations that may require further transportation and infrastructure evaluation.

---

## 💡 Recommendations

- Evaluate **Bogotá** more closely as a potential candidate for transportation infrastructure analysis
- Extend the analysis using multiple years of data
- Include additional variables such as public transportation investment and mass-transit usage
- Validate the comparability of economic indicators between countries before making investment decisions
